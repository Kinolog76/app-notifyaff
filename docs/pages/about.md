# Technical Specification (TS)

## Page: About NotifyAff

---

## 1. Page Purpose

The **About** page explains who is behind NotifyAff, why the product exists, and what values drive its development.

Primary goals:

- Build **trust and credibility**
- Strengthen **brand identity**
- Support **SEO** with branded and trust-related queries
- Answer the question: _“Why should I trust NotifyAff?”_

Secondary goal:

- Soft conversion (lead users to sign up)

---

## 2. Target Audience

- Affiliate marketers
- Bloggers & content creators
- Media buyers
- SaaS founders and teams
- Potential partners

---

## 3. SEO Requirements

### 3.1 Primary Keywords

- about NotifyAff
- affiliate link monitoring software
- affiliate tracking SaaS
- affiliate marketing tools

### 3.2 Meta Data

- **Title:** About NotifyAff — Affiliate Link Monitoring You Can Trust
- **Description:** Learn why NotifyAff was built, our mission, and how we help marketers protect affiliate revenue with real-time link monitoring.

### 3.3 Heading Structure

- One **H1** per page
- Logical structure: H2 → H3
- Brand name "NotifyAff" included naturally

---

## 4. Page Structure

### 4.1 Hero Section

**Goal:** Introduce the brand and mission

**Content:**

- H1: `About NotifyAff`
- Subtitle:

  > Built to protect affiliate revenue through reliable link monitoring

**UI / UX:**

- Clean layout
- Subtle animation (fade-in)

---

### 4.2 Origin Story

**Goal:** Explain why the product was created

**Content:**

- H2: `Why NotifyAff exists`
- Text block:

  - Affiliate links break silently
  - Existing tools focus on clicks, not reliability
  - NotifyAff was built to solve this exact problem

Tone: honest, practical, founder-driven

---

### 4.3 Mission & Vision

**Goal:** Communicate long-term intent

**Content:**

- H2: `Our mission`

- Mission statement:

  > Help marketers and businesses never lose revenue due to broken affiliate links

- H3: `Our vision`

  > A web where monetization links are always monitored and transparent

---

### 4.4 What Makes NotifyAff Different

**Goal:** Differentiate from competitors

**Content (bullet points or cards):**

- Focus on link reliability, not just analytics
- Real-time alerts instead of delayed reports
- Simple setup, no technical overhead
- Built specifically for affiliate use cases

---

### 4.5 Who We Build For

**Goal:** Reinforce product-market fit

**Content:**

- Affiliate marketers
- Bloggers & publishers
- Media buying teams
- SaaS and content platforms

### 4.6 Product Principles

**Goal:** Show values and quality standards

**Content:**

- Speed over complexity
- Transparency in monitoring
- Privacy & data protection

### 4.7 Trust & Transparency

**Goal:** Increase confidence

### 4.8 Call to Action

### 4.9 Footer

## 5. Animations & UX

- Subtle scroll-based animations (Framer Motion)
- No aggressive motion (trust-focused page)
- Support `prefers-reduced-motion`

---

## 6. Tailwind CSS Guidelines

- Clean typography-focused layout
- Max width for text readability (`max-w-3xl / max-w-4xl`)
- Neutral color palette
- Consistent spacing

## 8. Success Metrics

A trust-focused, SEO-optimized About page built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and subtle animations, reinforcing the NotifyAff brand and increasing user confidence.
